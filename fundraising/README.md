## 3. Warm-intro strategy

Cold forms work poorly for first-time founders; use them as the fallback, not the plan.
1. **Customers first.** A design partner who says "this recovered us $X" is the best introduction. Ask each partner who else in their network has the problem.
2. **Operators in the space.** Offer a free leakage read-out to a freight-audit or TMS industry operator; ask for two introductions after delivering value.
3. **Program route.** Accelerators and Innovation Norway introductions create credibility and connect you to investors who already trust the program.
4. **Existing portfolio founders.** Look at the public portfolio of each target firm; request an introduction from a founder through a normal professional channel and a short forwardable blurb.
5. **Content with real data.** Publish a short, honest piece (once measured) on detention recovery rates. Credible numbers attract inbound interest.
6. **Sequence.** Do not pitch until G3. Meet 2-3 friendly operators first to rehearse; then approach 15-25 firms in a batch so the fundraising window is short.

## Hiring principles
1. **Domain credibility beats AI credibility.** The hardest gap is someone who has lived inside freight audit, accessorial billing, or carrier accounts-receivable. The AI work is the easier hire.
2. **Hire against gates.** Roles 1-2 now; roles 3-4 after G1/G2 evidence (see `business/business-plan.md`).
3. **Small team, high trust.** 3-4 people through the seed. Contract or fractional for legal, accounting, and security.
4. **Equity.** Early-employee and founder equity is a conversation for counsel and the cap table; common early-stage ranges exist but were not researched here (grade C if cited).

## Role 1: Operator / CEO (Norway-based option)

**Why:** Someone must own sales, customer discovery, fundraising, and operations. For a founder in Norway, the pragmatic structure is a **Norway-based operator/CEO who sells to US customers remotely** and travels, with a US entity and advisor network (structure to confirm with counsel).

**Responsibilities:** customer discovery with 10-20 carriers/shippers; design-partner recruitment; pricing and contract terms; investor relations; hiring; budget and gates.

**Must have:** proven B2B sales or operations in a back-office, logistics, finance, or payments-adjacent domain; comfort with data/security conversations; fluent English; willingness to sell to US customers across time zones; ability to run a fundraise.

**Strong plus:** prior freight audit, TMS, 3PL, broker, carrier-finance, or accounts-payable software experience; US customer network; prior startup experience.

**Norway notes:** Norwegian employment law, working-hours norms, and tax/social costs differ from the US and affect offers and equity structure; get local legal and accounting advice (open item). A US-time-zone overlap schedule should be agreed from day one. Option: candidate in Norway for the CEO role, with the first US-facing sales hire or advisor on the US side later.

## Role 2: Founding engineer (LLM and document pipelines)

**Why:** build the pipeline in `technical/overview.md`, starting with the eval harness.

**Responsibilities:** gold-set and pass^k harness; ingest/extract/rules/evidence stages; verifier; audit trail; security basics; integration prototypes.

**Must have:** shipped production software with LLM extraction and document handling; test and eval discipline; strong Python or TypeScript; comfort owning a stack alone.

**Strong plus:** EDI or TMS/ELD API experience; OCR; fintech or compliance-grade audit trails.

## Role 3: Freight audit / accessorial domain lead (first customer-facing hire)

**Why:** write and validate the rule base; talk credibly to carriers and shippers; be the human reviewer during pilots.

**Responsibilities:** accessorial rule library; reviewing claims during pilots; maintaining claim deadlines and carrier/shipper conventions; onboarding customers; measuring recovery and cost per claim.

**Must have:** hands-on freight audit, carrier billing, or logistics claims experience; detail orientation; clear writing.

**Strong plus:** US carrier or shipper network; comfort with spreadsheets/SQL.

## Role 4: Customer success and recovery operations analyst (hire after G3)

**Responsibilities:** process live claims with human approval, follow up on disputes, report outcomes, feed learnings back to rules and evals.

**Must have:** accounts-receivable/collections or claims processing experience; organized; strong written communication.

## How to source

**Official job and talent channels**
- Post the roles on major job boards and the company website; in Norway, use the country's widely used job portals and the official public employment service listings (confirm which apply). Use LinkedIn Jobs for a company page and role posts.
- Startup communities and programs (Antler Oslo, StartupLab and similar hubs, Innovation Norway events) have official talent boards and founder events.
- University engineering and business programs publish official career pages and host recruiting events.

**Recruiters**
- Engage a technology/executive search firm for the CEO/operator role once funding is secured; ask Innovation Norway, program managers, and portfolio founders which firms they would recommend. Agree terms in writing (retained versus contingent, fee percentage, guarantee period) with at least two quotes.
- For the freight domain lead, use a US logistics-focused recruiter or a staffing firm in supply chain; ask industry associations which firms they recommend.

**Warm introductions**
- Design partners and prospects: ask "who is the best person you have worked with on freight billing?" after delivering value.
- Industry associations (e.g., TIA, CSCMP, and local logistics associations): attend official events, post in member job boards, and ask for introductions through their official contact channels.
- Investors and advisors: ask for introductions to operators in their networks.
- Open-source and public technical communities for the engineer: public project contributions and talks, approached through normal professional channels.

**Process rules**
- Written scorecards per role with the must-haves above; the same questions for every candidate.
- Paid trial project (a few days) for the engineer and domain lead, scoped against the gold set.
- Reference checks only with the candidate's permission.
- Use only information candidates choose to share and public professional profiles; no private-life research.

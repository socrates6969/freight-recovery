About

This site belongs to scoup.ai, an early-stage project exploring whether documented, rules-based checks of accessorial, detention and container D&D charges are useful to mid-market shippers and small and mid-size importers. Carriers may follow later, through factoring and broker partners.

  • Stage: pre-product. The platform is built and tested in our own environment; it is not hosted for customers.
  • Customers, pilots, integrations, certifications: none.
  • Legal entity, address, organisation number: to be published here once registered; we will not list placeholders as facts.

The approach is deliberately cautious: show the arithmetic, separate confirmed from uncertain, keep a person in charge of every short-pay and dispute.

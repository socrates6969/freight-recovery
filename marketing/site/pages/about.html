This site belongs to scoup.ai, an early-stage project exploring whether documented, rules-based detention and accessorial recovery is useful to carriers and shippers.

  • Stage: pre-product. A software prototype exists; it is not hosted for customers.
  • Customers, pilots, integrations, certifications: none.
  • Legal entity, address, organisation number: to be published here once registered; we will not list placeholders as facts.

The approach is deliberately cautious: show the arithmetic, separate confirmed from uncertain, keep a person in charge of every claim.

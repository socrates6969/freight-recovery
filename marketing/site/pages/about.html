About

scoup.ai builds documented, rules-based checks of the charges that are hardest to verify on freight invoices: accessorials and detention on carrier and broker invoices, and demurrage and detention on container invoices. The checks show their arithmetic and their sources so that the person paying the invoice can verify every finding.

  • What is live: the browser app, free and without an account. It runs the same rules engine that the team workspace uses, entirely on your device.
  • What is a prototype: the hosted team workspace (saved claims, document import, approvals, exports, API, QuickBooks reference data). It is built and tested in our own environment and is not offered to customers yet.
  • Customers, certifications, integrations: none are claimed.
  • Legal entity and organisation number: not confirmed for publication.

The approach is deliberately cautious: show the arithmetic, separate confirmed from uncertain, keep a person in charge of every short-pay and dispute, and never decide what is legally owed.

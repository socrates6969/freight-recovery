Privacy

Short version: the website collects nothing, the app sends nothing, and the only personal data we ever receive is what you put in an email to us.

The website

These pages are static. They contain no forms, no account registration, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no embedded third-party content. They run no scripts, set no cookies and use no browser storage.

The app

The app at /app/ runs in your browser. After it loads, its content security policy forbids every network connection, so the figures you enter cannot be transmitted to us or to anyone else. It sets no cookies and uses no browser storage; closing the tab discards everything. Downloads (packets, findings files, draft letters) are created on your device and never pass through a server.

Hosting

The site and the app are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process the information needed to deliver web requests, such as IP addresses, and describes its handling in the GitHub General Privacy Statement at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. We do not control, and do not receive, those hosting logs.

Email

If you write to the contact address, we receive the address and the message you send and use them to reply and to follow up on what you asked. We do not add you to a mailing list and do not share your message. Do not email invoices, shipment records or other customer data; the app exists so that you never have to.

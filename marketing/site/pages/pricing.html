How we plan to charge later

From the business plan. None of this is on offer today, and every figure below is an assumption written down before any customer data exists.

  1. Phase 1

    Contingency

    A share only of money a customer actually gets back, and nothing if nothing comes back, so an unproven vendor carries the risk. The plan models 20%; audit firms are said to charge 15 to 30%, a vendor-stated range we have not verified.

  2. Phase 2

    Platform fee plus lower contingency

    Assumed at $1,000 to $2,000 a month plus about 10 to 12%, for more predictable revenue.

  3. Phase 3

    Self-serve SaaS

    For teams that want the tools for their own staff, priced on loads processed.

Source: scoup.ai business plan, business/business-plan.md, section 4 (Pricing: contingency, with a path to SaaS), in the freight-recovery repository.

What free means here

The tools are free because they cost nothing to run: the rules execute on your device and the pages are static files. Nothing on this site tracks you and nothing you enter reaches us, so there is no data to monetise either.

The product, rule by rule

Three browser tools that share one rules engine, and a hosted team workspace that is still a prototype. Everything below is fixed logic you can check by hand; no model decides an amount, and no accuracy figure is published because none has been measured on real customer documents.

Freight invoice check

Live in the browser app

Inputs: the invoice lines (description and amount), the rate confirmation terms (linehaul, fuel surcharge, detention free hours, hourly rate and cap, authorized accessorials) and, optionally, the appointment, arrival and departure times from the bill of lading or gate record. Choose the shipper's or the carrier's perspective.

RuleWhat it flags
Duplicate lineIdentical description and amount billed more than once.
Linehaul rateA linehaul line that differs from the agreed rate by more than the tolerance.
Fuel surchargeA fuel line that differs from the agreed surcharge.
Unauthorized accessorialAn accessorial line whose name is not among the authorized accessorials; listed for human review because a receipt may support it.
Total mismatchAn invoice total that does not equal the sum of its lines.
Detention unbilled, under-billed, over-billedDetention recomputed from the time records: early arrival starts the clock at the appointment, free time is deducted, billable time is rounded down to the increment, and the hourly rate and cap are applied. The difference to what was invoiced becomes the finding.

Findings are split into confirmed (counted in the pre-review estimate) and needs human review (listed, never counted). Each finding carries its rule, its fixed confidence setting and its calculation steps. The packet (Markdown), the findings file (JSON) and the draft letter (text) are created on your device.

Ocean D&D audit

Live in the browser app

Inputs: the invoice fields 46 CFR 541.6 requires, each container with its charged periods, your own evidence (clock dates, cargo availability, dates a return was refused), and optionally a public carrier tariff preset to recompute against. A CSV import with the same columns loads a whole invoice at once.

CheckWhat it does
Part 541 appliesWhether the invoice date falls on or after the rule's effective date (28 May 2024).
Required invoice content (541.6)Which required items are missing or inconsistent, with the clause quoted.
30-day issuance (541.7)Whether the invoice was issued within 30 calendar days of the charge last incurred, or, for an NVOCC, of the invoice it received.
Invoice totalWhether the total due exceeds the sum of the charge lines.
Line arithmeticWhether each charged period equals days times the stated rate.
Tariff recomputationFree time and chargeable days under the chosen public tariff (working or calendar days, tiers, rates by container size) compared with what was charged.
Cargo availability (545.5)Whether free time was counted from before the cargo was available.
Refused empty return (545.5)Detention days that fall on dates when the empty container could not be returned.

Tariff presets are public figures from carriers' US tariff documents as published on the retrieval date shown in the app, with their source and verification level; your service contract or a newer tariff may differ. The audit surfaces dates and required fields for review; it does not decide whether a charge is legally owed. Confirm current rules and any dispute with counsel or your customs broker.

Sources: Electronic Code of Federal Regulations, 46 CFR Part 541, current through 2026-10-15, https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-541; Federal Maritime Commission, "U.S. Court of Appeals Issues Decision in Case on Demurrage and Detention Billing Practices", https://www.fmc.gov/articles/u-s-court-of-appeals-issues-decision-in-case-on-demurrage-and-detention-billing-practices/, accessed 2026-10-19.

Charge names

Live in the browser app

Carriers name the same charge in many ways, and a charge under an unfamiliar name is easy to skip. The tool shows two views of each name you paste: the fixed-rule class the invoice check uses (detention, linehaul, fuel, accessorial, other) and the nearest of 256 charge names from seven public carrier tariffs and the U.S. GSA uniform rules tariff, with a similarity score. A suggestion is only shown above a fixed threshold, and it only adds an item for review; it never confirms an amount.

Team workspace

Prototype, early access

The hosted workspace runs the same engine with saved claims and a team around them: document import with every extracted field confirmed by a person, revisions and timelines, four-eyes approval, a hash-linked audit log, team charge labels, fuel surcharge checks, exports, an API with scoped keys and QuickBooks Online reference data. It is built and tested in our own environment, has processed no customer data, and is not hosted for customers.

Limits

  • The browser tools work from the figures you enter; they do not read PDFs.
  • Rules are fixed and general. Your contract may define detention, free time or accessorials differently; the findings are the starting point for that conversation, not its end.
  • No results on real invoices are published. Internal benchmark figures are not published and have not been independently reproduced.
  • No external security assessment yet. See security and privacy.

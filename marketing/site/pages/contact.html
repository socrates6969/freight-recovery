Become a design partner

We are validating whether this is worth building. If you work on a shipper's transportation, logistics or AP team, or handle container D&D invoices at an importer, we would like to hear from you.

What we propose

  • You share 90 days of paid invoices, read-only: carrier and broker invoices with their rate confirmations and BOL or gate records, or container D&D invoices with the carrier tariff.
  • We run the prototype and give you a free review of your own invoices, with the evidence packets: which lines the documents support, which they do not, and which need a person to decide.
  • Nothing is disputed or short-paid without your approval.
  • You tell us where the rules are wrong or the contracts differ. That feedback is what we are after.

Carriers, and factoring or broker firms that work with carriers, are welcome to write too; that side comes later.

Before any real documents are exchanged we will agree handling terms (NDA, retention, deletion) in writing. Please do not email real shipment documents in the first message.

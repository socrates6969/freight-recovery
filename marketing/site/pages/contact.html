There is no form and no tracking on this site. Write to __CONTACT_EMAIL__.

Reasons to write

  • A rule is wrong for your contracts. Tell us which finding, which contract term, and what the right result is. That feedback shapes the rules.
  • Early access to the team workspace. Include your company, your role, roughly how many carrier, broker or D&D invoices you handle a month, and the systems you use. The workspace is a prototype and is not hosted yet.
  • A vulnerability report. Describe what you found and how to reproduce it. Please do not include anyone else's data.
  • Carriers, factoring and broker firms: welcome too. The freight check already works from the carrier's side.

Please do not email documents

Do not send invoices, rate confirmations, shipment records or any customer data by email. Use the app instead: it runs in your browser and nothing you enter reaches us. Any future exchange of real documents would need agreed handling terms and a suitable transfer process first.

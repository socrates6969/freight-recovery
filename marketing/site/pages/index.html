The problem

Detention and accessorial charges are often billed without proof, disputed without evidence, or paid without checking them against the rate confirmation. Carriers write off time they were not paid for; shippers pay lines that were never authorized. Industry research (for example from ATRI) describes detention as a significant cost of waiting. We quote no figures here until we have checked them against the primary source, and a cost of waiting is not the same thing as a pool of recoverable dollars.

What it does

  1. Bring one load's documents. Rate confirmation, BOL or gate record, and the invoice.
  2. Rules apply the contract terms. Clock start, free time, rate and cap, with every step of the arithmetic shown.
  3. Get an evidence packet and a DRAFT demand. Per-document SHA-256 fingerprints, confirmed findings separated from items needing review, and a letter marked DRAFT / not sent.

Four cost areas, one evidence trail

  • Truck detention: waiting time beyond free time, computed from the contract terms and time records.
  • Accessorial charges: invoice lines checked against the rate confirmation, with charge names recognised across carriers' different wording.
  • Fuel surcharges: recomputed from the official weekly diesel price and your own surcharge table.
  • Ocean container fees: demurrage and detention invoices checked against the US billing rules and the carrier tariff.

Every finding goes into the same evidence packet and needs a second person's approval. See every module and how it is tested.

Who it is for

  • Carriers: bill earned detention with the proof attached instead of writing it off.
  • Shippers and brokers: catch unauthorized or unsupported accessorials and wrong-week fuel surcharges; ambiguous lines are flagged for human review, not auto-disputed.
  • Importers: check container demurrage and detention invoices against the rules before paying them.

What we do not do

  • We never send anything without human approval.
  • Only confirmed findings count toward the recoverable estimate; low-confidence items are listed separately.
  • We do not move money, and we do not give legal advice.

Questions

Is this live?

No. It is pre-product software in design-partner validation. There is no hosted service and no customer data is accepted.

Which systems does it connect to?

None yet. The platform reads documents you provide (PDF, CSV, text) and has a programming interface; there are no TMS, ELD or accounting integrations.

What does it cover?

Truck detention, accessorial charges on freight invoices, fuel surcharges and ocean container demurrage and detention. The Platform page lists each module and how it is tested.

How is it priced?

The intended pilot model is a contingency on dollars actually recovered. Rates are not final and nothing is for sale yet.

Is my data safe?

We currently only work with synthetic data. See the security status page for what exists today and what is still required before any real data.

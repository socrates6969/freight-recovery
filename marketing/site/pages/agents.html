The agent team

Five agents, one invoice, every line

A freight audit is split into five agents. Each has one job, a defined input and output, and its own tests; the hand-off between them is fixed. Language models read documents and draft text, fixed code decides every amount, and a person approves before anything leaves.

Status, plainly: the contract and tariff lookup, the checks and the evidence packet run today in the browser app as fixed rules on your device. Reading documents and the independent reviewer are in development and not live. No accuracy figure is published, because none has been measured on real customer documents.

01

Intake

In development

Turns the paperwork for one load into structured facts: the invoice lines, the rate confirmation terms, and the appointment, arrival and departure times from the bill of lading or gate record.

Input

Carrier or broker invoice, rate confirmation, bill of lading or gate record; for ocean D&D, the demurrage or detention invoice.

Output

Invoice lines (description, amount), contract terms and timestamps, each with where it came from.

  • Today, in the browser app: you type or paste the figures, or import a CSV for an ocean D&D invoice. Nothing is read automatically.
  • Team workspace prototype: PDF text, CSV and text are parsed in an isolated worker and every field is confirmed by a person.
  • Planned: a language model reads messy documents, each value carries a pointer to its page and span, and low-confidence values are flagged for a person instead of guessed.
02

Contract and tariff lookup

Live in the browser app

Finds the terms that should govern each charge, so the next agent has something to compare against.

Input

The invoice lines from intake and the agreement you hold: a rate confirmation for truckload, a carrier tariff for ocean.

Output

For each line, the agreed rate, allowance or clause, or a note that none exists.

  • Linehaul rate, fuel surcharge, detention free hours, hourly rate and cap, and the list of authorized accessorials.
  • For ocean D&D, the content and timing rules of 46 CFR Part 541 and, if you choose one, a public carrier tariff preset to recompute against.
  • Charge names: each name is classed by fixed rules and matched to the nearest of 256 public tariff charge names (Recall), so a charge under an unfamiliar name is not skipped. Suggestions are for a person to review.
03

Re-rating and audit checks

Live in the browser app

Recomputes every line from the agreed terms with exact decimal arithmetic and records each difference as a finding, with its steps.

Input

Invoice lines and the governing terms.

Output

Findings, each confirmed (counted in the pre-review estimate) or needing human review (listed, never counted), with the calculation.

04

Evidence and claim drafting

Live in the browser app

Puts every finding, its arithmetic, the governing term and the invoice values it rests on into an evidence packet, and drafts the dispute letter.

Input

The findings and their sources.

Output

Evidence packet (Markdown), findings file (JSON) and a letter marked DRAFT, for human review, not sent, not legal advice.

  • Built on your device from the values on screen; the app never sends anything.
  • For ocean invoices, the regulation or tariff clause is quoted next to each finding, and the letter states the request date from the invoice date.
05

Reviewer

In development

Checks every flag before it is reported. The rule is simple: no source, no claim.

Input

The draft packet and the source documents.

Output

Each statement passed with its source, or blocked; then a person approves.

  • Today: confirmed findings are kept apart from items that need a person, and only confirmed ones are totalled.
  • Team workspace prototype: four-eyes approval before any letter leaves, and a hash-linked audit log.
  • Planned: an independent verifier, separate from the drafter (a different prompt and, where practical, a different model), that checks each sentence against the cited source span and rejects uncited or contradicted claims.

Rules the agents keep

  • Models read, code decides

    No language model computes an amount or decides entitlement. Those come from versioned, readable rules.

  • No citation, no claim

    A statement that cannot be traced to a source is not reported.

  • A person approves

    Nothing is sent, short-paid or disputed by the software.

  • No money movement

    scoup.ai never handles payments; corrections flow through the carrier's own billing.

  • Untrusted input

    Emails and PDFs are treated as untrusted input; the test plan includes documents with hidden instructions and tampered timestamps.

  • Measured before claimed

    Quality bars are set before measurement, on held-out data; no result is published until it is measured.

Ocean D&D and the FMC rule

The lookup and check agents apply the content and timing provisions of 46 CFR Part 541. Requirements and deadlines depend on the billing party and the facts; confirm current rules and any dispute with counsel or your customs broker.

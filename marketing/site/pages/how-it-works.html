How it works

Four steps, all on your device. The example below is synthetic and is the one the app loads with "Load synthetic example".

1. Enter the figures

From the carrier or broker invoice: the charge lines, as description and amount. From the rate confirmation: the linehaul rate, the fuel surcharge, the detention terms and the authorized accessorials. From the bill of lading or gate record: appointment, arrival and departure times. For an ocean D&D invoice, the fields the invoice must carry, each container's charged periods and, if you have it, your own clock evidence; or import a CSV.

2. The rules run in your browser

The page is a static bundle. After it loads, its content security policy forbids every network connection, so the figures stay on your device. The rules use exact decimal arithmetic and fixed settings (detention rounding increment, amount tolerance, dispute window), all shown in the settings panel.

Worked example (synthetic load LD-5001). The invoice bills Linehaul $1,500.00, Fuel Surcharge $168.00, Detention $300.00 and Lumper $150.00. The rate confirmation says linehaul $1,400.00, fuel $168.00, 2 free hours of detention at $50.00 per hour up to 8 hours, and authorizes only Detention. The gate record shows appointment 08:00, arrival 07:45 and departure 11:30.

  • Linehaul: $1,500.00 billed against $1,400.00 agreed, a $100.00 overcharge.
  • Fuel surcharge: matches the rate confirmation, no finding.
  • Detention: the truck arrived early, so the clock starts at the 08:00 appointment; 3 h 30 min on site, minus 2 free hours, is 1 h 30 min, rounded down to the 15-minute increment, times $50.00 is $75.00. The invoice bills $300.00, so $225.00 is over-billed.
  • Lumper: not among the authorized accessorials, so it is listed for human review rather than counted, because a receipt may support it.

Each of those steps appears under its finding, and in the packet, so you can check them by hand.

3. Review the findings

  • Confirmed findings are totalled as a pre-review estimate.
  • Needs human review findings are listed separately and never totalled.
  • Findings in the other direction (for example a charge the carrier under-billed, when you check from the shipper's side) are shown but not counted.
  • For ocean invoices, each finding cites the invoice values it rests on and quotes the regulation or tariff clause.

4. Download the packet and the draft letter

The evidence packet (Markdown) records the inputs, every finding with its arithmetic and the draft letter. The findings file (JSON) holds the same data for your own systems. The letter is marked DRAFT, for human review, not sent, not legal advice; the app never sends anything.

Ocean D&D invoices

The audit compares the invoice with the content and timing provisions of 46 CFR Part 541 and, if you choose one, a public carrier tariff. Section 541.8 gives the billed party at least 30 calendar days from issuance to request mitigation, refund or waiver; the draft letter states that date from the invoice date. Section 541.4 is reserved following a court decision, and deadlines vary by billing-party type. The audit does not decide whether a charge is owed; confirm current rules and any dispute with counsel or your customs broker.

Sources: Electronic Code of Federal Regulations, 46 CFR Part 541, current through 2026-10-15, https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-541; Federal Maritime Commission, "U.S. Court of Appeals Issues Decision in Case on Demurrage and Detention Billing Practices", https://www.fmc.gov/articles/u-s-court-of-appeals-issues-decision-in-case-on-demurrage-and-detention-billing-practices/, accessed 2026-10-19.
